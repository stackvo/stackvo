# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other side is acting in good faith and knows things you do not.

Concretely, these are not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment, insults about who somebody is rather than what they wrote, publishing anyone's private information, and sustained disruption of a discussion after being asked to stop.

Disagreement is not on that list, and should not be. This project's own documents argue with earlier versions of themselves; a review that finds a claim wrong is the most useful thing anybody can bring. Criticise the work as hard as it deserves — the line is between the work and the person.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where somebody is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **backend@cyh.com.tr**.

Reports are read by the maintainer and nobody else. You will get an acknowledgement; you may not get a detailed account of what was decided, because the other person's side of it is not the reporter's to be told.

## What happens then

There is no committee and no appeal process, because there is one maintainer and pretending otherwise would be a procedure nobody can actually run. What there is:

- **A private word**, for something that reads as a bad day rather than a pattern.
- **A public correction**, when the behaviour was public and left standing would look endorsed.
- **A block**, for harassment, for anything targeting a person rather than the work, or for a pattern that continues after being named.

Enforcement is a judgement call made by one person, which is a real limitation of this arrangement and is stated rather than dressed up. If you think a decision was wrong, say so — by email, and it will be reconsidered.
