certificate warning. Four short steps, in order:

Already using XAMPP, Laragon, MAMP, Valet, Sail, Herd or DDEV? StackVo can
bring your projects over — see [Coming from another tool](migrating.md).

## What you will have at the end

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **A workspace** | One folder, `~/.stackvo` by default, holding your settings and projects. |
| **A project** | Its own container, its own runtime version, its own database. |
| **An address** | `https://<name>.loc`, with a certificate your browser trusts. |
| **A window** | Start, stop, rebuild, logs, mail, snapshots — no terminal needed. |
